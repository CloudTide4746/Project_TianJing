## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are cultural tourism visitors, exhibition audiences, and general digital collectible enthusiasts.
- They use the app on mobile while visiting ruins, reviewing photos afterward, or sharing generated collectibles with friends.
- Their main job is to turn a ruin photo into a meaningful, ceremonial digital artifact they can keep and share.

### Brand Personality
- Quiet, refined, ceremonial.
- The interface should feel trustworthy, poetic, and culturally rooted rather than trendy or game-like.
- The product should reduce interaction confusion while preserving a sense of ritual during creation and sharing.

### Aesthetic Direction
- White ink-wash direction with elegant negative space, paper texture, restrained contrast, and soft ink diffusion details.
- Favor a gallery-like presentation that feels light, calm, and premium for mainstream cultural tourism audiences.
- Keep every major screen, including the generating screen, inside the same bright visual system.
- Avoid glossy dark cyber aesthetics, loud gradients, heavy glassmorphism, and generic card dashboards.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with calm hierarchy: use clear reading order, generous spacing, and restrained accents.
2. Make ritual feel simple: preserve ceremonial moments through motion and copy, not visual clutter.
3. Let content breathe: prioritize imagery, titles, and stories with strong whitespace and quiet surfaces.
4. Use ink as emphasis: apply brush-like strokes, borders, and tonal shadows sparingly for focus.
5. Keep interaction obvious: every core action must stay easy to understand for mainstream mobile users.
